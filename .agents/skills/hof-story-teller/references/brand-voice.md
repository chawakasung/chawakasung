# Hall of Frame — brand voice

## The brand core

A hall of fame of paintings, made ownable. The painting is already famous; the frame is what
turns it into an object in someone's home. Everything Hall of Frame sells sits at that join.

Three things follow from it, and they settle most copy decisions:

1. **The buyer is not being taught who Van Gogh is.** Fame is the premise, not the pitch.
   Art-history exposition is the most common way this copy goes wrong.
2. **The frame is the product.** It is where the craft, the choices, and the price live. Copy
   that could be pasted under a bare poster has not done its job.
3. **The wall is the outcome.** Nobody buys a print. They buy the corner of a room that is
   currently blank.

## Register

Warm, plain, specific. A well-made shop, not a gallery wall text and not an ad.

- **Confident, not reverent.** The painting does not need protecting.
- **Concrete over adjectival.** "กรอบไม้สีวอลนัท ขอบ 3 ซม." beats "กรอบสวยหรู".
- **Short sentences.** Marketplace is read on a phone, one thumb, half-attention.
- **Honest about what it is.** Saying "งานพิมพ์" clearly builds more trust than it costs.
- **No exclamation marks.** One per listing at absolute most, and only in the ask.

## Vocabulary

**Use** — words that carry information:

| Thai | English |
|---|---|
| งานพิมพ์คุณภาพสูง | fine art print |
| กรอบไม้ / กรอบอลูมิเนียม | wood frame / aluminium frame |
| ขอบกรอบ … ซม. | frame profile … cm |
| ขนาดรวมกรอบ | overall framed size |
| พร้อมแขวน / มีอุปกรณ์แขวนให้ | ready to hang |
| ผิวสี / เนื้อสี / รอยแปรง | surface, pigment, brushwork |

**Avoid** — words that either mislead or say nothing:

- `ภาพต้นฉบับ`, `ออริจินัล`, `ของแท้`, "original", "authentic" — see fidelity rule 1. These
  are the dangerous ones.
- `limited edition`, `รุ่นพิเศษ`, `หมายเลข …` — only if a real numbered run exists.
- `หรูหรา`, `พรีเมียม`, `ระดับโลก`, `masterpiece quality` — adjectives standing in for specs.
- `ชิ้นสุดท้าย`, `ราคาจะขึ้น` — only if true.
- `เติมเต็มทุกพื้นที่`, "elevate your space" — filler that appears in every competitor listing.

## How much art history is allowed

Two sentences, maximum, and only if they change how the buyer sees the object in front of
them. Useful: what the painter was doing when they made it, what the colour was reacting to,
why this image outlasted the others. Not useful: birth and death dates, a career summary,
a museum's catalogue number.

Anything you would have to look up, look up — do not write from vague memory. Nothing in this
repo verifies art-historical claims, and general web fetching is blocked (see `CLAUDE.md`),
so `WebSearch` is the tool that works here.
