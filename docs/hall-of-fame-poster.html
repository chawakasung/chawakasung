Forty people,
ten traits,
one wall.

Every persona in this skill carries the same ten scores. Read alone the numbers say little — almost everyone scores high on rigor, almost nobody on risk. So this sheet plots each person against the roster instead: every bar is one trait’s distance from the group average, measured in standard deviations, drawn up or down from the centre line.

The tallest upward bar is a person’s signature trait — not what they are best at, but what they are most unusual for. That trait decides which bench they stand on below. Pick a trait from the scale to trace it across all forty.
